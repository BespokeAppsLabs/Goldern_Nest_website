### Market Trends and Drivers

**1. Post-HPAI Market Restructuring**
The 2023 Avian Influenza outbreak fundamentally changed market dynamics, creating a premium for reliable, biosecure suppliers and eliminating smaller, less resilient producers.

**2. Consumer Preference Shifts**
There is a growing consumer demand for high-quality, traceable protein. Consumers are willing to pay a premium for consistency, safety, and reliability.

**3. Retail Channel Evolution**
Large wholesalers and supermarket chains are consolidating their supply chains, seeking high-volume, professional partners like Golden Nest who can guarantee consistent supply.
