- **System Width:** 1.54m per row

**Materials & Protection:**
- Cage sheet metal: Galvanized or zinc-aluminum alloy coated

**Feed & Water Systems:**
- End set feeding with 0.75 KW MPF drives
- CHAMPION chain feeding system
- Water supply with ball tank system
- Additional drinking nipples with D28 nipple tube
- Top nipple orange drinkers
- Integrated rinsing system

### 2. Manure Management System
**Belt System:**
- HD2 type manure belts with 0.75 KW drive
- 1mm thick manure belts
- Support system for pressure shaft
- Manure belt idler as idler auger

**Removal System:**
- Cross manure conveyor: 500mm width, 14m length
- Vertical conveyor: 13m length at 23° angle
- Discharge height: 2.94m above ground
- Conveying capacity: 60 tonnes/hour
- Maximum removal interval: 4 days
- Includes motor cover and manure chute

### 3. Egg Collection System
**EggCellent Longitudinal Collection:**
- Capacity: 19,000 eggs per hour
- Elevator height: 2.1m transfer to cross conveyor
- Individual 0.37 kW motors per elevator
- 4-tier simultaneous collection capability
- Rod conveyor with cleaning function
- Individual belt cleaners per tier

**Cross Collection System:**
- 500mm wide curve conveyor
- Capacity: 50,000 eggs per hour
- Total length: 27m
- 3 pivoting units
- 14 floor supports (BD 2000 complete)
- GRP covers for 15m conveyor and 3 pivoting units
- 12m section with dirt collecting boxes

### 4. Water Management System
**Connection Unit:**
- 1" electrical preassembled unit
- Flow rate: 500-6,500 L/h

- Water meter and pressure reducer
- Medicator bypass connection

**Medicator System:**
- Type: 0.2-2.0% medicator

**Water Consumption Estimates (per house):**
