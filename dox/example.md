### Batteries

Lithium ion batteries are the most practical type of batteries to use on electric vehicles. They have the highest energy density per kilogram (the amount of electrical power it can hold, per kilogram of the battery weight) and a high cycle life compared to other batteries. There are several types of lithium ion batteries available with different voltage and different capacity.

The Chemistry of the Lithium ion battery determines what the best use for the battery is. Some of the chemistry design in batteries are fairly new but out dated as a new Lithium ion battery designs and chemistry proved to be better. There is a variety of choices on lithium ion batteries and the application determines the best type of battery to use.

| Battery Type | Abbreviation | Specific Energy | Common Usage |
|--------------|-------------|-----------------|--------------|
| Lithium Cobalt Oxide | LiCoO2 (LCO) | 200Wh/Kg | Cell phones, MP3 players (outdated) |
| Lithium Manganese Oxide | LiMn2O4 (LMO) | 150Wh/Kg | Power tools, medical devices |
| Lithium Nickel Manganese Cobalt Oxide | LiNiMnCoO2 (NMC) | 220Wh/Kg | Electric Vehicles, Medical tools, Cell phones (preferred) |
| Lithium Iron Phosphate | LiFePO4 (LiPo) | 120Wh/Kg | Solar systems, portable energy storage |
| Lithium Nickel Cobalt Aluminium Oxide | LiNiCoAlO2 (NCA) | 260Wh/kg | Electric Vehicles, Power tools (preferred for EVs) |

#### Electric Vehicles

The cell type used on the EV with the highest range (Tesla Model S) is the 21700 NMC. One cell of this battery is 21mm in diameter and 70mm long, and the Tesla Model S can achieve a range of 539km on a single charge. The vehicle has around 7000 of these cells arranged in a manner that will achieve the best kilowatt hour (kWh) rating. The problem with these batteries is that they are not easily available as the design is rather new. The 18650 NCA battery cell has an advantage over the 21700 NMC as it is more readily available while having a high specific energy. They are 18mm in diameter and 65mm long. The 21700 NCA is about three times as expensive which is why we decided to use the 18650 NCA cells for our initial conversions.

Battery modules will be arranged in different configurations depending on the application. The battery to be used has a voltage of 3.7v and a minimum capacity of 3000mAh. Depending on the parallel and series connection of the battery cells, we can increase the voltage and current of our battery modules.

#### UPS Systems

The type of batteries used in the UPS system will be determined by what the system is used for. The UPS system will be available in different sizes ranging from 400Wh to 10kWh. With this much power, the UPS can supply power to a fridge for at least 8 hours or power a home entertainment system for more than 10 hours. It will support three and two pin 30amp sockets. It will also have USB and 12V socket output.

### Motor

The drive unit used on the converted vehicle will be a three phase Induction AC motor. This is preferred as it can save energy during operations compared to other motor types. This type of motor can also generate an electric charge which can be used to recharge the battery. The power of the motor will be determined by the application on the converted vehicle. Larger vehicles like busses and trucks will need a larger, more powerful motor than that of small ones like sedan cars and motorcycles.

The motor can be connected to a customised gearbox made for electric drive trains or can be coupled directly to the drive shaft of the vehicle. 4 wheel drive vehicles can be fitted with 4 or 2 motors depending on the application of the vehicle.

### Inverter & Chargers

An inverter is a device used to convert DC current from the battery to AC current which is used on normal appliances. It will be used as a connection between the battery pack and appliances or the motor in the converted EV. An Inverter is sometimes called an AC drive as it regulates and controls the speed or torque of the motor. The size of the inverter will be determined by the power requirements of the client.

Different types of chargers will be used to charge different products. Super chargers are used to fast charge electric vehicles while home wall chargers can charge the vehicle over an extended period of time. Solar chargers are used in charging the batteries of grid tied or off grid solar systems and can also be used to charge the UPS system.

### Super Capacitors

A Super Capacitor functions like a battery but can recharge about ten thousand times faster than a normal battery and has a lifespan 1000 times that of a normal lithium ion batteries (1 million cycles). They cannot practically replace batteries but can be used in conjunction with a battery pack to make the system more efficient. They are typically used for high current charging and discharging making them ideal for storing the energy generated by the motor while braking. The high current discharge is ideal for high power accelerations in EVs and appliances that require high currents (grinders welding machines). Using super capacitors will increase the overall range of the battery packs in EVs.

Super Capacitors are ideal in converting vehicles that require a high current to power the components such as fork lifts and grading equipment. These equipment can benefit from a super capacitor-battery system.

Super Capacitors can be used in UPS systems where high current is needed. Places like Hospitals, Butcheries and large grocery shops where fridges and other equipment need a high current.
