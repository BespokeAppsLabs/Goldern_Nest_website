## Part 1: July 2025 Retrospective Analysis

### Original Challenge: The 16,000 Chicken Mystery
Initial analysis of July spreadsheet data suggested a massive inefficiency:
- **Assumed:** 16,000 chickens producing 15,520 eggs daily
- **Reality:** Only R99,982 recorded revenue
- **Calculated Waste:** 73.2% of eggs unsold (highly unrealistic)

### Solution: Reverse Engineering the Truth
Through detailed analysis, we discovered:
- **Actual July Flock:** Started with 3,200 chickens
- **Growth Pattern:** Added 3.4 chickens daily
- **Final July Count:** 3,302 chickens
- **True Revenue:** R170,000 (including unrecorded R70,018)

### July Operations Model
```
Starting Flock: 3,200 chickens (July 1st)
Daily Growth: +3.4 chickens
Final Flock: 3,302 chickens (July 31st)
Average Daily Production: 3,154 eggs
Sales Efficiency: 95% (5% inventory buffer)
Total Monthly Production: 97,786 eggs
Total Monthly Sales: 92,897 eggs
```

### Inventory Management Strategy (July)
The dramatic sales variations in the spreadsheet were explained by sophisticated inventory management:

**High Sales Days (Inventory Drawdowns):**
- July 3rd: 10,787 eggs sold (used 7,832 from storage)
- July 29th: 8,306 eggs sold (used 5,266 from storage)

**Low Sales Days (Inventory Building):**
- July 16th-19th: Building reserves for peak demand
- July 26th: Continued storage accumulation
